Skip Clips Uploader

Skip Clips Uploader is a private tool used by Skip to the Good Part, a page that posts short clips from podcasts.

It saves the video clips we make into our own Google Drive, so they are kept safely and can be opened on our phones to post, and it uploads them to our own YouTube channel as private videos that we review and publish by hand. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

It asks Google for the narrowest Drive permission there is: it can only see and change files it created itself, never anything else in the Drive.
