Privacy Policy

Skip Clips Uploader ("the app") is a private tool used only by Skip to the Good Part to save its own video clips to its own Google Drive and to read the view counts of its own TikTok videos. Last updated 18 October 2026.

What the app accesses

The app uses one Google permission, drive.file. With it, the app can create files and folders in the Google Drive of the account that signed in, and read, change or delete only the files and folders it created. It cannot see any other file in that Drive.

What the app does with it

It uploads video clips and cover images that we produce, lists the files it uploaded, and checks how much Drive storage is in use. Nothing else.

TikTok

A second private tool reads the view counts of our own TikTok videos through TikTok's official Display API, after the owner of the @skiptothegoodpartclips account signs in with TikTok Login Kit and allows the user.info.basic and video.list permissions. It reads only that account's video IDs and view counts, for our internal reporting. It does not post, comment or message, and it does not collect data about any other TikTok user. The sign-in token is kept only in our own private settings. Access can be removed at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and websites.

Sharing and storage

The app does not sell, share or transfer any data to anyone. It does not use data for advertising. The sign-in token Google issues is kept only in our own private settings and is never published. Files stay in our Google Drive.

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google account's settings: myaccount.google.com/permissions.
